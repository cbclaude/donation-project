Our Mission

Source: woundedwarriorproject.org/mission

"To honor and empower wounded warriors. To foster the most successful, well-adjusted generation of wounded service members in our nation's history."

Who We Serve

Wounded Warrior Project® (WWP) is the nation's leading veterans service organization, dedicated to the total well-being of post-9/11 wounded, ill, or injured veterans and their families. From mental health and physical wellness to VA benefits assistance, peer support, and more, we're changing the way our nation cares for veterans and helping them thrive for a lifetime.

Post-9/11 wounded, ill, or injured veterans and their families are eligible to join WWP and never pay a penny for our services and support. Those who are currently on active duty, transitioning, or served on or after September 11, 2001, may be eligible. A disability rating is not required.

Our Commitments

  • Provide the most critical services to warriors most in need by assessing their challenges and prioritizing the programs we offer while improving their quality of life.
  • Expand program partnerships with nonprofit, for-profit, and government agencies.
  • Build connections and strong communities as a foundation for warrior well-being.
  • Offer health care programs that are comprehensive and tailored to each warrior's unique needs.
  • Advocate through innovation, leadership, and advocacy to keep our promise to honor and empower those we serve.

Recognition & Accountability

WWP holds a 2025 Platinum Seal of Transparency from Candid (formerly GuideStar). Wounded Warrior Project has a four-star rating — the highest possible — from Charity Navigator. We are a Better Business Bureau (BBB) accredited charity that has met all standards for accountability.

More than 70% — over $263 million in fiscal year 2024 — was spent directly on programs and services for wounded warriors, families, and caregivers.

Warrior Care Network®

Source: woundedwarriorproject.org

Warrior Care Network is WWP's partnership with four leading academic medical centers to pioneer clinical trauma care and research for veterans.

About Warrior Care Network

Established in 2016, Warrior Care Network® is a $100 million initiative that includes four PTSD treatment centers located in Atlanta, Boston, Los Angeles, and Chicago. The network provides access to high-quality care for veterans dealing primarily with post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and traumatic brain injury (TBI).

In 2024, WWP invested an additional $100 million to fund programs for post-9/11 veterans that aim to reduce depression, anxiety, and substance use disorders.

Treatment Approach

  • Group therapy with other veterans to learn coping strategies in a peer environment
  • Evidence-based treatments for PTSD, TBI, substance abuse disorder, and military sexual trauma
  • Holistic wellness programming including yoga and meditation
  • Clinical research to improve outcomes for future veterans
  • Partnerships with leading academic medical centers for cutting-edge care

Treatment Centers

Atlanta, GA

Emory Healthcare Veterans Program — specializing in PTSD and TBI treatment through evidence-based therapies.

Boston, MA

Home Base Program at Massachusetts General Hospital — a national center of excellence for veterans and military families.

Los Angeles, CA

UCLA Operation Mend — intensive outpatient programs for veterans and their families dealing with unseen wounds.

Chicago, IL

Rush University Medical Center — trauma-focused care combining clinical treatment with community support.

Program Impact

Programs like Warrior Care Network are contributing to a declining rate of suicide among former service members. According to research, social support among veterans has been associated with better quality of life and serves as a protective factor against suicidal thoughts.

In 2024, WWP provided more than 68,600 hours of treatment for warriors coping with unseen wounds.

Alumni Connection Program

Source: woundedwarriorproject.org

"If I can be a part of an organization that helps people heal and find peace, then the end of my service is not the end. I'm just serving in a new way now." — WWP Warrior

About the Alumni Connection Program

The Alumni Connection Program brings together Wounded Warrior Project alumni — warriors who have participated in WWP programs and services — to form a powerful community of veterans who support each other and give back to those still on their journey of recovery.

WWP believes that those who were once carried can and will become a pillar of strength for others. The Alumni Connection Program embodies this belief, creating a cycle of service where healed warriors become mentors and advocates for those still in need.

What Alumni Do

Peer Mentoring

Alumni warriors mentor newer members, sharing their experiences and providing peer-to-peer guidance through the recovery journey.

Advocacy

Alumni advocate on behalf of all veterans at the local, state, and national level, amplifying the WWP mission in their communities.

Community Events

Alumni organize and participate in connection events, fundraisers, and outreach efforts that strengthen the veteran community.

Fundraising

Alumni raise funds for WWP programs through events like the Carry Forward 5K, helping ensure future warriors receive the same support they did.

Carry Forward 5K

The Carry Forward 5K is WWP's signature fundraising event, giving supporters a tangible way to put the mission in motion. Thousands of warriors, alumni, and supporters participate each year — running, walking, or rolling to raise funds and awareness for wounded veterans.
